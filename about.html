About WanderTours

We’re on a mission to make extraordinary travel experiences accessible to everyone, everywhere.

Our story

WanderTours was founded with a simple belief: the best travel moments come from connecting with local experts who know a place by heart.

What started as a small curated list of day trips has grown into a global platform featuring thousands of experiences across more than 2,500 destinations.
